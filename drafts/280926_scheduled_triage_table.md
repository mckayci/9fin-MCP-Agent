## Notes and Data Caveats

1. **Entrant list interpretation:** The report does not publish an explicit "entrants to the distressed and restructuring watchlist" table. The fixed-rate stressed bond entrants (X Corp, Kernel, Pagaya, Arxada, CoreWeave) and stressed loan entrants (Rovensa, Paysafe) were used as the entrant list, per orchestrator instruction. Three of these (X Corp, Pagaya, CoreWeave) are US / non-European credits. They were kept because the brief said to take the lists as published with no region filter.
2. **Expected-restructurings losers table** (Merlin, Emeria) was not added as a separate source. Both names are already captured through the loan and bond losers tables.
3. **Report data points to check:**
   - Modulaire's watchlist-loser row shows no prior-week price or weekly change, although it appears in the 1pt-or-more losers table.
   - Rovensa's TLB maturity is truncated to "9 September 203..." in the entrants table, against 29-Sep-2030 in the loan losers table and Sep-2030 in the 9fin cap table.
   - Biscuit International's TLB maturity is shown as 15-Feb-2030 in the report, against Aug-2030 post-A&E in the 9fin cap table.
4. **Virgin Media (loan, 9fin company 938) and Virgin Media - O2 (bonds, 9fin company 1626)** are treated as one credit group for this screen.
5. **Stale 9fin data:** X Corp (2021/22), Rovensa (cap table Jun-25, financials FY20), Selecta (Jul-25 restructuring PF), Accell (Dec-24), Howden (Sep-25), Planet (no financials or cap table), Metinvest (no cap table). Figures are shown as at the stated date and have not been rolled forward.
6. **Continuity:** None of the 22 names appears in `/target_list/target_list.csv`, so there are no carry-forward views. For context, Emeria was named alongside Cerba (target list, "Further work", 26-Sep-2026) in a Les Echos piece on French companies facing the 2028 debt wall (Jul-26).
