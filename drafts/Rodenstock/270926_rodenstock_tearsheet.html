Business Description

Munich-based manufacturer of ophthalmic lenses, founded 1877, c.5,000 employees, six production sites, sold through opticians and retail chains in 85+ countries. Built around premium individualised lenses: the B.I.G. VISION biometric range (610 patents; Fitch: 40% of core sales from highly differentiated products) and MyCon myopia-control lenses for children (+62% sales, 2024). Strong profitability versus peers — 61% gross margin, 22% Fitch-adjusted EBITDA margin — from proprietary technology and lower-cost manufacturing in Eastern Europe/Asia. Sold its eyewear division to De Rigo in Dec-2022, leaving a lenses-only business. Small versus EssilorLuxottica, Hoya and Zeiss, constraining Fitch's competitive-positioning score to 'bb-'.

Corporate history: second period of lender-level stress. Sold by founders to Permira (2003) then Bridgepoint (2006); 2010-11 standstill on EUR370m debt. Compass Partners control 2016-2021. Apax bought the group from Compass in Apr-2021 (EV EUR1.275-1.5bn, c.10.2x 2021 EBITDA) alongside Mubadala (~24%), backed by a EUR610m equity cheque. Facilities amended and upsized May-2022. Apax has been exploring a refinancing since Mar-2025; none has completed (confirmed directly, and consistent with Fitch's 06-Jul-2026 report, which rates the existing structure and conditions the rating on refinancing within six months).

Situation Overview

The EUR840m TLB (Jun-2028) and EUR125m RCF (Dec-2027) have not been refinanced as of 27-Sep-2026. Apax has been trying since Mar-2025; in that time leverage has risen to 8.5x, FCF has turned negative, the RCF has been drawn to 71%, and the CEO has left with no successor named.

FY2024's modest improvement (revenue +1.2% to EUR512.4m, EBITDA +5.1% to EUR128.6m, company basis) did not continue. Fitch reports FY2025 sales of EUR493m (-3.8%) and a 22.0% Fitch-adjusted margin (implies c.EUR108m EBITDA, below the EUR125m the 2021 buyout was marketed on), driven by a soft German market (31% of sales, consumers trading down) and the GrandVision contract phase-out. Fitch expects a further low-single-digit sales decline in 2026 before 3.0-3.2% growth resumes, with the margin recovering to 24.4% on cost savings already executed — but its own leverage forecast (8.0x in 2026, "towards" 7.7x in 2027) stays above its 7.5x downgrade trigger throughout.

No deleveraging or sponsor equity support is on record since 2021. Despite constrained liquidity the company still made a small Croatian bolt-on in Mar-2026 (terms undisclosed). Moody's withdrew its Caa1 rating at the issuer's request (30-Jul-2026), three weeks after Fitch's B- assignment — not tied to any completed transaction. The loans trade at 93.2 (25-Sep-2026), down from the mid-90s pre-downgrade but stable since June: the market is pricing refinancing risk, not a distressed outcome.

Key Catalysts / Triggers to Watch
Maturity
EUR965m senior secured stack (EUR125m RCF due Dec-27, c.15 months; EUR840m TLB due Jun-28, c.21 months) confirmed NOT refinanced. Fitch's rating is conditioned on a refinancing, or credible plan, within six months of 06-Jul-26 (window closes c.early Jan-27). Fitch's own rating case assumes refinancing only happens in 2027, at a 6% margin vs 5% today — itself evidence the agency does not expect near-term completion.
Leverage
8.5x EBITDA leverage at end-2025 (Fitch), forecast 8.0x (2026) and "towards" 7.7x (2027) — both above Fitch's own 7.5x downgrade sensitivity. FY2025 margin (22.0%) sits only 2pp above Fitch's 20% downgrade trigger.
Covenant
RCF springing covenant test is live. 9.5x net leverage test triggers at ≥40% drawn (EUR50m of EUR125m); RCF was 71% drawn (EUR89m) at end-2025. No breach reported; covenant-basis headroom not confirmed (Fitch's gross 8.5x sits within c.1.0x of the 9.5x trigger).
Liquidity
Constrained: c.EUR48m accessible (EUR12m unrestricted cash + EUR36m undrawn RCF) at end-2025, under 10% of FY25 sales. FCF "particularly weak" in 2025; Fitch flags possible downgrade to CCC on underperformance, working-capital swings or higher capex.
Governance
CEO vacant since 30-Jun-26 (Desimoni departed for personal reasons, no successor named); CFO in post only since Feb-2026. Refinancing execution now rests with a leadership team with under 8 months' combined tenure in their current roles.

Recommendation: Add to Restructuring Watchlist

All three triggers are present. Maturity wall: EUR965m of senior secured debt (RCF due Dec-27, TLB due Jun-28) remains unrefinanced after 18 months of sponsor-led effort; Fitch's own six-month refinancing window closes c.early Jan-2027, and its rating case does not expect a deal before 2027, at a higher (6%) margin. Liquidity: only c.EUR48m accessible at end-2025, FCF negative, Fitch flags possible downgrade to CCC. Covenant: the RCF's 9.5x springing test is live (71% drawn); no breach reported but headroom unconfirmed. Execution risk is compounded by a vacant CEO seat and a CFO eight months into the role leading the refinancing.

Fitch's own recovery analysis implies c.48% recovery on the senior secured stack in a distressed scenario (RR4) — well below the current 93.2 trading price, meaning the market has not yet priced that outcome. Next steps: watch for a refinancing launch, amend-and-extend, or sponsor equity commitment before early Jan-2027; the CEO appointment; FY2025 audited accounts; and Fitch's review at the end of its six-month window.
