Business Description

Ahlstrom (formerly Ahlstrom-Munksjo) develops and manufactures fiber-based specialty materials including filtration media, food and consumer packaging papers, medical fabrics, release liners, and building materials. The company operates globally with over 50% of sales derived from non-cyclical, resilient end-markets such as life sciences and food packaging.

Taken private by Bain Capital in 2021, alongside co-investors Ahlstrom Invest B.V. (36.4%) and Viknum AB (8.6%). The company has since restructured from five to three operating divisions and executed selective asset disposals, including the 2022 sale of its Decor business for over EUR 200m. In 2025, it acquired US-based Stevens Point to expand its North American food and consumer packaging footprint.

Situation Overview

EBITDA has improved sequentially (EUR 504m LTM vs EUR 473m FY25), driven by procurement and operational excellence initiatives alongside a mix shift toward higher-margin specialty materials. Q2 2026 sales grew 5.6% year-on-year with EBITDA up 15.9%.

However, net leverage increased from 4.7x in FY24 to 5.5x at FY25 following the fully debt-funded Stevens Point acquisition, since described as "aggressive" by rating agencies. Fitch downgraded the issuer to B (stable) from B+ (negative) in March 2026. Management is targeting leverage below 4.0x within the next year through EBITDA growth and strict cost/capex discipline.

Bloomberg reported in August 2025 that Bain Capital was considering an EUR 8bn sale or Amsterdam IPO. The sponsor has gradually extracted cash (EUR 41m in FY22, EUR 38m in FY23, EUR 34m in FY24). No exit has yet materialised.

Key Catalysts / Triggers to Watch
Maturity
EUR ~610m SSNs maturing Feb 2028 (EUR 350m @ 3.625% fixed + $305m @ 4.875% fixed). The USD TLB due Feb 2028 was refinanced in Jul 2026 via a $545m fungible add-on due 2030. Management states 80% of the debt structure is now extended to 2030 and "there is no rush" to refinance the remaining SSNs.
RCF
Springing RCF maturity clause: a EUR 28.5m RCF tranche matures Aug 2027, but if more than EUR 500m of existing senior secured term debt remains outstanding in Nov 2027, the EUR 362m RCF tranche (maturing Nov 2029) is brought forward to Jan 2028. This creates a hard deadline for the SSN refinancing.
Leverage
Net leverage 5.2x (IFRS 16) / 4.4x (company adjusted). Interest coverage 2.1x (IFRS 16). Persistent net losses (EUR 169m LTM) driven by high depreciation/amortisation (EUR 282m) and financial expenses (EUR 239m). FCF generation modest after capex and interest.
Liquidity
Liquidity EUR 502m: cash EUR 173m + undrawn RCF EUR 391m - letters of credit EUR 61m. Supplemented by EUR 216m non-recourse factoring and EUR 160m supply chain financing. Adequate for near-term operations but tight relative to the 2028 maturity wall.

Recommendation: Add to Restructuring Watchlist

Ahlstrom has made meaningful progress in addressing its maturity wall, extending 80% of its debt structure to 2030 through successive A&E transactions. However, approximately EUR 610m in senior secured notes maturing Feb 2028 remains outstanding. A springing maturity clause on the RCF creates a hard deadline: if these SSNs are not refinanced before Nov 2027, the EUR 362m RCF tranche accelerates to Jan 2028, compressing liquidity materially.

Net leverage remains elevated at 5.2x (IFRS 16), interest coverage is thin at 2.1x, and the company has generated persistent net losses. The debt-funded Stevens Point acquisition delayed the deleveraging path. While management is targeting sub-4.0x leverage and operating performance is improving, the refinancing window is narrowing. Bain Capital's exit ambitions add further uncertainty to the capital structure trajectory.

Primary triggers: (1) Unrefinanceable maturity wall: EUR 610m SSNs due Feb 2028 with RCF springing clause. (2) Leverage: 5.2x net with thin interest coverage and limited FCF after debt service. Monitor for refinancing activity on the remaining SSNs and the trajectory of EBITDA through H2 2026.
